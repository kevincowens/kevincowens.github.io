# Lessons Learned

## Unit 1 - Introduction & Mindset

### What did I learn?

As this was the first unit, I did not know what to expect. I knew that it was best practice to have a positive mindset when thinking about oneself. Imposter Syndrome is hard to overcome, however. I have known it numerous times as I have progressed through my career. Most recently, when I took the position of First Sergeant.

I was only filling in while we waited on our official First Sergeant to arrive. I did not have the extensive training that First Sergeants go through, so I did not think that I would be able to fill the role. After some time, and after being able to assist my Airmen when they had a need, I fell into a groove and realized that being a First Sergeant was the best position I have filled in my 22 years in the military.

### How will you use it moving forward?

The confidence that spending time as a First Sergeant has given me has helped me develop my growth mindset. I now know that I can fill any role that comes my way. I just need to believe in myself and work towards being the best at whatever position I find myself in.

### What are your key takeaways?

The concepts that I will take away from this unit are two-fold: I am aware of what Imposter Syndrome is, and that having a growth mindset is the key to overcoming it and improving myself in whatever role I am in.

## Unit 2 - Goals & Motivation

### What did I learn?

In this unit, I learned to take a mindful look at what my values are. The main value that sticks out to me is Integrity. It means being honest and moral, no matter the circumstances. It is something that I strive to live out every day.

Something that I already knew, but was brought to the forefront of my mind, was that the driving motivator in everything I do is my family. They are the reason that I get up and go to work every morning, and they are the reason that I have started taking college courses again. I am motivated, because I want to give them the best life that I can.

### How will you use it moving forward?

Knowing that my goal is to give my family the best life I can, I have the motivation to keep going forward with my college courses. Doing this will lead to getting a bachelor's degree, which will afford me better job opportunities once I retire from the military.

### What are your key takeaways?

My key takeaways from Unit 2 are that my motivations and goals are inextricably linked. I was aware that I did everything for my family, and that is why I was going back to college. I did not realize just how intertwined the two were, how one built up the other, which then built up the other. It opened my eyes regarding why I do what I do each and every day.

## Unit 3 - What are Your Big Rocks?

### What did I learn?

Unfortunately, this week was probably the most disheartening. When I saw that there was an entire unit devoted to time management, I got excited. I came into it hoping for tools or techniques that would give me more hours in the day. However, because I have gamed this system for as long as I have, I have squeezed everything I can out of it. Even the time that I listed in the assignment as "TV" time is time that I am also at my computer working on schoolwork.

One positive from this unit is that it was around this time that I started improving my workflow. I began working with Markdown formatting, which is what this assignment is written in. It allowed me to write my notes in a standardized format, making them more organized. It also allowed me to create links between notes that contain overlapping concepts, which helps reinforce the material. Although this did not save me much time, it did help me to better organize my thoughts, which made me feel less stressed about the outcome of these courses.

### How will you use it moving forward?

I will continue working on my time management skills, trying to find ways to improve my efficiency and gain back some of my time. I will also continue to hone my workflow, which will assist me with passing future courses.

### What are your key takeaways?

My takeaways were that I am currently doing everything I can to manage my time, but there are still improvements I can make in how I go about my studying process.

### What are your key takeaways?

While initially intimidating, an interview is just two people talking. As long as I go into the interview prepared, I will be fine.

## Unit 8 - Lessons Learned

### What did I learn?

Unit 8 is all about self-advocacy and communications. I am good at one of these, not so much the other. Having been in the military for as long as I have, I have learned how to communicate in a professional manner, both verbally and in writing. I have been doing it so long that it sometimes bleeds into personal communications. My wife has pointed out to me on more than one occasion that I will sometimes text in my "military voice".

Self-advocacy is the one that I need to work on. I tackle all of my tasks with the mindset that I can accomplish anything that I set my mind to. While I know that is a mostly true statement, it is not universal. There are things that I will have to do in my life that will require me to seek out assistance from others. I need to make sure that I can recognize those times and properly request the help.

### How will you use it moving forward?

I will be more mindful of times that I may be in need of some external assistance and actually ask for it. This is one that will take time and deliberate work to improve on.

### What are your key takeaways?

My main takeaway is that it is okay to ask for help.
